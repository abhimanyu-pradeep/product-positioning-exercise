## Positioning Statement

**Acme Dispatch** is a **field service management platform** that brings your team's jobs, invoices, and customer communication into one place, with route optimization, real-time crew tracking, and automated follow-ups. Unlike spreadsheets and phone calls, **Acme Dispatch gives the owner full visibility into every job and every dollar** — without needing IT staff. We set it all up for you.

## Market Category & Style

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Field Service Management Platform |
| **Style** | Big Fish, Small Pond |
| **The Pond** | 5-20 person trades businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) |
| **Expansion Path** | Trades → Property maintenance → General field services |

## Competitive Alternatives

- **Most common:** Whiteboard schedule + phone calls + paper invoices. Owner drives around checking on crews.
- **Sometimes:** Generic tools duct-taped together — Google Calendar + QuickBooks + WhatsApp group.
- **Occasionally:** Enterprise FSM (ServiceTitan, Jobber) — but $300+/mo and takes weeks to set up.

| Job-to-invoice in one tap | Yes | Manual | 3 separate apps | Yes |

| Route optimization at SMB price | Yes | No | No | Yes ($$$) |

## Value Themes

1. **Know where your money is.** See revenue per crew, per job type, per neighborhood.
2. **Stop losing jobs to chaos.** Every job tracked, every follow-up automated, nothing on the whiteboard.
3. **Get paid faster.** Invoice from the job site, payment link via text, auto-reminders.
4. **No IT person needed.** We configure everything — you focus on running crews.

## Target Customer

- Trades business (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control)
- 5-20 crew members across 2+ trucks
- Owner or ops manager runs scheduling directly
- Outgrown the whiteboard — jobs getting missed, invoices delayed
- **Trigger:** Added the 3rd or 4th truck. Can't keep track by driving around anymore.

## Trend

Homeowner demand for same-day service is rising (40% expect response within 4 hours). Trades businesses that can't route and dispatch efficiently lose to those that can — and the customer reviews compound.
